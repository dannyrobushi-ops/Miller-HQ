POLICY RUBRIC
Version 2026-10-27 / text analyzer 0.1. This is a transparent editorial framework and an experimental local text-matching engine, not a trained or validated statistical language model. Anchors and rationale are available in the site and export. -2/+2 are relative endpoints on the stated US policy rubric, not an objective extremism measure. Many issues are multidimensional. Contradictory directional evidence is shown as mixed, not zero. Same-direction anchors show their range and midpoint. Duplicate canonical source URLs and dates count once. Counts describe evidence breadth, not confidence probabilities. No temporal decay or automatic change-of-position inference is implemented.
